Unit 1: Are you here on vacation?

Greetings, Countries, Travel Vocabulary, and Verb to be

English – Second Intermediate – First Semester 1448H

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

The Big Idea

How do you introduce yourself and ask about someone's country in English?

In this unit, we will learn how to greet people, introduce ourselves, ask about countries and nationalities, and use travel and tourism vocabulary. We will also master the Verb "to be" (am, is, are) in present tense.

What You Will Learn

Greetings: Hello, Hi, Good morning, How are you?

Countries: Saudi Arabia, USA, UK, Egypt, etc.

Nationalities: Saudi, American, British, Egyptian, etc.

Travel Vocabulary: vacation, tourist, passport, airport

Grammar: Verb to be (am, is, are)

"Hello! My name is Ous. I am from Saudi Arabia. Are you here on vacation?"

First: Greetings and Introductions

Greetings: Words we use when we meet someone. Introductions: Telling someone your name and information about yourself.

Formal Greetings

  • Good morning! (صباح الخير)
  • Good afternoon! (مساء الخير)
  • Good evening! (مساء الخير)
  • Hello, how are you?
  • Nice to meet you.
  • How do you do? (رسمي جداً)

Informal Greetings

  • Hi! (أهلاً)
  • Hey! (هاي)
  • What's up? (ماذا يحدث؟)
  • How's it going? (كيف الحال؟)
  • How are you doing?
  • Long time no see!

Ahmed: Good morning! My name is Ahmed. What's your name?

John: Good morning! I'm John. Nice to meet you.

Ahmed: Nice to meet you too. How are you?

John: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

Ahmed: I'm great, thanks!

How to Introduce Yourself

1 Start with a greeting

"Hello!" / "Good morning!" / "Hi!"

2 Say your name

"My name is..." / "I am..."

3 Ask about the other person

"What's your name?" / "How are you?"

4 Respond politely

"Nice to meet you." / "I'm fine, thanks."

✅ Always be polite and smile when greeting someone!

Responses to "How are you?":

  • Positive: "I'm fine, thanks." / "I'm great!" / "Very well, thank you."
  • Neutral: "I'm okay." / "Not bad."
  • Negative: "I'm not so good." / "I'm tired."

Asking about Country and Nationality

1 Question: Where are you from?

Answer: I am from Saudi Arabia.

2 Question: What is your nationality?

Answer: I am Saudi. / I'm Saudi.

3 Question: Are you American?

Answer: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. I'm British.

4 Question: Where is he from?

Answer: He is from Egypt. He is Egyptian.

Fourth: Verb to be (am, is, are)

Verb to be: The most important verb in English! It means "يكون" in Arabic. We use it to describe who we are, where we are from, and how we feel.
I am | He/She/It is | You/We/They are
I

am

"I am Saudi."

"I am from Riyadh."

He / She / It

is

"He is American."

"She is a tourist."

You / We / They

are

"You are welcome."

"We are from Saudi Arabia."

Pronoun Verb to be Short Form Example
I am I'm I am a student. / I'm a student.
You are You're You are kind. / You're kind.
He is He's He is my friend. / He's my friend.
She is She's She is from Egypt. / She's from Egypt.
It is It's It is a hotel. / It's a hotel.
We are We're We are tourists. / We're tourists.
They are They're They are from Japan. / They're from Japan.

Using Verb to be in Different Ways

1 Positive Sentences (الجمل المثبتة)

"I am from Saudi Arabia."

"She is a tourist."

"We are on vacation."

2 Negative Sentences (الجمل المنفية)

"I am not (I'm not) from the USA."

"He is not (He isn't) British."

"They are not (They aren't) tourists."

3 Questions (الأسئلة)

"Am I from Egypt?" → "Yes, you are. / No, you aren't."

"Is she American?" → "Yes, she is. / No, she isn't."

"Are they tourists?" → "Yes, they are. / No, they aren't."

✅ Remember: am → I | is → he/she/it | are → you/we/they
Questions with Verb to be:
Move the verb to the beginning!
"You are happy." → "Are you happy?"
"She is from France." → "Is she from France?"

Short Answers (الإجابات القصيرة):

  • "Are you Saudi?" → "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not."
  • "Is he American?" → "Yes, he is." / "No, he isn't."
  • "Is she Egyptian?" → "Yes, she is." / "No, she isn't."
  • "Are they tourists?" → "Yes, they are." / "No, they aren't."

Let's put everything together! Read this dialogue and practice it with a partner.

Eighth: Common Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1: Using the wrong verb to be

❌ Wrong: "I is from Saudi Arabia." / "She are American."

✅ Correct: "I am from Saudi Arabia." / "She is American."

Remember: I → am | He/She/It → is | You/We/They → are

Mistake 2: Confusing country and nationality

❌ Wrong: "I am from Saudi." / "I am Saudi Arabia."

✅ Correct: "I am from Saudi Arabia." / "I am Saudi."

Country = place name | Nationality = adjective

Mistake 3: Forgetting "the" before some countries

❌ Wrong: "I am from USA." / "She is from UK."

✅ Correct: "I am from the USA." / "She is from the UK."

Use "the" with: the USA, the UK, the UAE, the Philippines

Mistake 4: Wrong word order in questions

❌ Wrong: "You are from Egypt?"

✅ Correct: "Are you from Egypt?"

In questions, the verb comes BEFORE the subject.

Mistake 5: Using "a" before nationalities incorrectly

❌ Wrong: "I am a Saudi." (when describing origin)

✅ Correct: "I am Saudi." OR "I am a Saudi student."

Use "a" only when nationality is followed by a noun.

Mistake 6: Pronunciation of "vacation"

❌ Wrong: "va-ka-tion"

✅ Correct: "vay-KAY-shun" /veɪˈkeɪʃən/

Practice saying it: "I am on vacation."
