The Big Idea

How do you introduce yourself and ask about someone's country in English?

In this unit, we will learn how to greet people, introduce ourselves, ask about countries and nationalities, and use travel and tourism vocabulary. We will also master the Verb "to be" (am, is, are) in present tense.

What You Will Learn

Greetings: Hello, Hi, Good morning, How are you?

Countries: Saudi Arabia, USA, UK, Egypt, etc.

Nationalities: Saudi, American, British, Egyptian, etc.

Travel Vocabulary: vacation, tourist, passport, airport

Grammar: Verb to be (am, is, are)

First: Greetings and Introductions

Greetings: Words we use when we meet someone. Introductions: Telling someone your name.

Formal Greetings

  • Good morning!
  • Good afternoon!
  • Good evening!
  • Hello, how are you?
  • Nice to meet you.

Informal Greetings

  • Hi!
  • Hey!
  • What's up?
  • How's it going?
  • How are you doing?

Ahmed: Good morning! My name is Ahmed. What's your name?

John: Good morning! I'm John. Nice to meet you.

Ahmed: Nice to meet you too. How are you?

John: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

Ahmed: I'm great, thanks!

How to Introduce Yourself

1 Start with a greeting

"Hello!" / "Good morning!" / "Hi!"

2 Say your name

"My name is..." / "I am..."

3 Ask about the other person

"What's your name?" / "How are you?"

4 Respond politely

"Nice to meet you." / "I'm fine, thanks."

✅ Always be polite and smile when greeting someone!

Asking about Country and Nationality

1 Question: Where are you from?

Answer: I am from Saudi Arabia.

2 Question: What is your nationality?

Answer: I am Saudi. / I'm Saudi.

3 Question: Are you American?

Answer: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Using Verb to be in Different Ways

1 Positive Sentences

"I am from Saudi Arabia." / "She is a tourist."

2 Negative Sentences

"I am not from the USA." / "He isn't British."

3 Questions

"Are you a tourist?" → "Yes, I am. / No, I'm not."

"Is she American?" → "Yes, she is. / No, she isn't."

✅ Remember: am → I | is → he/she/it | are → you/we/they

Seventh: Common Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1: Using the wrong verb to be

❌ Wrong: "I is from Saudi Arabia."

✅ Correct: "I am from Saudi Arabia."

Mistake 2: Confusing country and nationality

❌ Wrong: "I am from Saudi." / "I am Saudi Arabia."

✅ Correct: "I am from Saudi Arabia." / "I am Saudi."

Mistake 3: Forgetting "the" before some countries

❌ Wrong: "I am from USA."

✅ Correct: "I am from the USA."

Use "the" with: the USA, the UK, the UAE, the Philippines

Mistake 4: Wrong word order in questions

❌ Wrong: "You are from Egypt?"

✅ Correct: "Are you from Egypt?"

Mistake 5: Using "a" before nationalities incorrectly

❌ Wrong: "I am a Saudi." (when describing origin)

✅ Correct: "I am Saudi." OR "I am a Saudi student."
